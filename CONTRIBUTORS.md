# Contributors

BiasedDoom grows through more than code. This file recognizes the people who
shape the project from the player's side — reporting subtle glitches,
suggesting features and polish, and stress-testing releases — without
necessarily sending commits or pull requests. Their insights land in the
engine all the same, and they deserve to be named.

If you have sent feedback that led to a change, you belong here.

## Community Contributors

  menu options), proposed the Doom 64-inspired **Absolution** fog preset, and
